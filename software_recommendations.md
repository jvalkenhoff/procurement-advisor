## Recommendation

No plan fits the full budget of 300 EUR/month and supports all requirements ("Internal knowledge base", "Single sign-on (SSO)", "Document collaboration", and "Access permissions"). 

- The closest fit is **Notion Plus** at 285.0 EUR/month which fits the budget but does not support SSO or Access permissions.
- **Confluence Standard** is also within budget at 159.53 EUR/month and supports all requirements except SSO, which is unknown.
- For full support of all requirements, the plans with SSO supported (Notion Business, Confluence Enterprise, Notion Enterprise) are either over budget or have unknown pricing.

If strict budget adherence is mandatory, Confluence Standard or Notion Plus are recommended with the caveat of missing or unknown SSO support. If full feature support is critical regardless of budget, further inquiry for Enterprise plans pricing is advised.
